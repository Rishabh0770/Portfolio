About

Websites that look good, load fast and bring customers

I’m Rishav Kumar, a web developer and digital marketer based in Solan, Himachal Pradesh. I build modern, responsive and performance-focused websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, PHP, Node.js, MySQL and MongoDB. My approach goes beyond design: every website should be fast, easy to use, search-engine friendly and created with a clear goal of turning visitors into customers.

Alongside development, I help businesses grow online with SEO, Google Business Profile, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads and social media marketing. I also support e-commerce growth, including product listings through Amazon Seller Central. From planning and launching a website to building its visibility and digital presence, I bring the essential pieces together under one roof.

I’ve worked with businesses and individuals across wellness, travel, healthcare, fitness and creative services. Currently, I’m helping Chimak Healthcare build Aiva Wellness into an online wellness brand through its e-commerce website, marketing and social media presence. With 2+ years of hands-on experience, I turn ideas into practical digital solutions designed to support real business growth.
